# What the research changed

Every change below follows a finding in [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md); the reasoning
and the retractions are in [`PROPOSAL.md`](PROPOSAL.md). Both cases pass their
full validator, and the ten sampled instances were re-drawn from the new
template.

## The Package Deal — `cases/package_deal.yaml`

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Issues | 6 (3,600 packages) | **7 (10,800 packages)** |
| Log-roll pair | `export_controls` × `security_commitment` | **`semiconductor_controls` × `critical_minerals`** |
| `precursors` | compatible | **distributive — a lever Beijing withholds** |
| `security_commitment` | widest issue on DELTA's sheet (range 40) | **low-range side issue (range 10)** |
| BATNAs | 45 / 30 | 46 / 30 — the 37%/23% asymmetry preserved |

**The log-roll was re-seated.** Both sides in fact rank export controls first, so
the shipped pair had *aligned* rather than opposed intensity. The record
documents a different pair: in July 2025 BIS rescinded EDA licence requirements
after Beijing resumed rare-earth magnet licensing (CRS R48642). Each ladder is
now drawn from the instruments the governments actually use — Entity List →
foreign direct product rule → performance thresholds → general licence →
rescission on one side; outright bar → case-by-case licensing → general licence
against end-use certification → suspension on the other. Membership is Tier A;
**orientation is Tier B**, because that exchange reversed a six-week-old
escalation and is equally readable as mutual de-escalation.

**`precursors` was re-priced.** Every source contradicted the compatible coding —
0% inter-source concordance, the firmest result in the review. Tariffs were
imposed over non-cooperation and halved at Busan to restore it. It is now exactly
zero-sum: Beijing holds the lever and sells it.

**`security_commitment` was demoted and re-laddered.** Anti-Secession Law Article
8 names three triggers for non-peaceful means and arms sales are not among them;
the USCC's 745 pages carry no arms-sales recommendation; the 2025 NSS frames
Taiwan around the Second Island Chain; Taiwan did not arise at Busan. The ladder
is now a **specific pending package — released, deferred, or held** — because
that increment is what the record shows being used as leverage, not the
relationship. *This is a claim about tradeability, not about value.*

## The Quarantine — `cases/quarantine.yaml`

Now the **flagship case**, because it is the better evidenced: CSIS and RAND
independently corroborate the premise, the inspection axis, the low walk-away
values and the direction of their asymmetry.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| BATNAs | 34 / 36 | **30 / 42** |
| `quarantine_scope` | operational stages | **cargo classes** |
| `deconfliction_channel` | 14/16 peak | **18/20 peak** |

**Walk-away gap widened.** RAND finds the PRC holds "effectively every advantage
over Taiwan", that a counterblockade "is unlikely to be successful", and that the
asymmetry *worsens as the quarantine runs*.

**Scope re-laddered by cargo class** — all cargo boarded → humanitarian exempt →
humanitarian and energy exempt → all civilian cargo exempt → regime lifted.
RAND: "there may be room to negotiate the movement of **particular kinds of
commodities**", not whether the quarantine ends.

**Hotline upgraded.** It was priced against Beijing's 2022 suspension. The MMCA
working groups of November 2025 and May 2026 are publicised approvingly by both
militaries' own outlets — a stronger compatibility signal than the hedge.

## Elsewhere

- **The Mandarin arm now carries a directional hypothesis.** The USCC records "a
  troubling divergence… between China's English-language and Chinese-language
  propaganda about Taiwan", domestic-facing material being markedly more
  escalatory. If models inherit it, `salient_zh` should shift them toward
  escalation relative to `salient`.
- **Bug fixed: the issue count was hardcoded.** Role sheets said "Each of the six
  issues" regardless of the case — so every Quarantine prompt ever run contained
  a scenario saying "Five issues" and a rule saying "six". Now derived from the
  case, in both languages.
- **Families versioned** (`package_deal_v2`, `quarantine_v2`) so runs against the
  old structure cannot silently pool with new ones.
- Ten instances re-sampled; five tests rewritten; 87 passing.
